SWARMIE — TYPOGRAPHY REFERENCE

Interface and marketing typeface: Google Sans
Weights: Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600, Bold 700
Runtime font: packages/config/fonts/GoogleSansVariable.ttf
License: packages/config/fonts/Google-Sans-OFL.txt
Shared font entry: @swarmie/config/fonts
Fallback: ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif
Code: the shared system monospace stack

The swarmie wordmark is canonical outlined artwork. Never recreate it by typing
swarmie in Google Sans or another typeface. Preserve the original geometry and
proportions of the approved SVG lockup.

Native typography follows its platform configuration. The brandkit does not own
another runtime font stack, density scale or component theme.
